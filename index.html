Frequently asked questions

What are the opening hours of Saffron Café & Kitchen?

Saffron Café & Kitchen is open every day from 8 am to 11 pm. We serve breakfast and coffee from 8 am to 12 pm, lunch and evening snacks from 12 pm to 7 pm, and dinner from 7 pm to 11 pm.

Is Saffron Café & Kitchen good for kids?

Yes. Families are welcome at every meal and we keep high chairs ready, so just mention one when you reserve. Children usually enjoy our pancakes with honey, sattu paratha, fresh lime soda and chocolate brownie, and the kitchen can make most dishes less spicy on request.

What type of cuisine does Saffron Café & Kitchen offer?

We combine café favourites with authentic Bihari food: specialty coffee from Coorg and Chikmagalur, all-day breakfast, and Bihari classics like litti chokha, Champaran mutton, sattu paratha and a full Bihari thali, alongside pasta and grilled chicken bowls.

Can I book a table at Saffron Café & Kitchen online?

Yes. On our table booking page, choose breakfast, lunch, dinner or a private party, then pick a date, time and number of guests. We call to confirm within the hour. You can also call +91 91220 60237 or simply walk in.

Does Saffron Café & Kitchen have vegetarian options?

Yes. Many of our dishes are vegetarian, including litti chokha, paneer butter masala, sattu paratha and the Bihari thali, and vegetarian food is cooked separately from meat dishes. Tell us about any allergies when you book and we will adjust your dish.

Where is Saffron Café & Kitchen located in Patna?

We are on Boring Road, Patna, Bihar 800001, easy to reach from Patliputra Colony, Bailey Road and Fraser Road, and a short drive from Gandhi Maidan and Patna Junction. Call +91 91220 60237 if you need directions.

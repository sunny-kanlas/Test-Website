Menu questions

How much is litti chokha at Saffron Café & Kitchen?

A plate of four litti with chokha costs ₹220, including taxes. It also comes in our Bihari thali (₹449) with dal, rice, seasonal sabzi, raita and thekua.

What does Champaran mutton cost?

Champaran mutton is ₹520. It is slow-cooked in a sealed clay handi with whole garlic and mustard oil, the way it is made in Champaran.

Which dishes on the menu are vegetarian?

Litti chokha, sattu paratha, paneer butter masala, the Bihari thali, pasta arrabbiata and all our desserts are vegetarian. Vegetarian food is cooked separately from meat dishes.

Do you serve brunch on weekends?

Yes. Breakfast and coffee run every day from 8 am to 12 pm, and on Saturdays and Sundays the kitchen keeps the brunch menu going until 1 pm.

Are taxes included in the menu prices?

Yes. Every price on this menu includes taxes, and we do not add a service charge.

The Story Behind Litti Chokha: A Bihari Delicacy

Saffron Café & Kitchen · November 2026

The origins of litti chokha

Litti chokha is a traditional dish from Bihar, known for its rustic flavours. It has been a staple in Bihari homes for generations and is loved for its simplicity and nutrition.

Ingredients and preparation

Litti is whole wheat dough stuffed with sattu (roasted gram flour) and spices, then baked or roasted. Chokha is a spicy mash of roasted brinjal, tomato and potato with mustard oil, garlic and green chillies.

Cultural significance

Litti chokha is served at festivals and family occasions, and stands for the resourcefulness of Bihari cooking: simple local ingredients turned into a satisfying meal.

Litti chokha at Saffron Café & Kitchen

We bake our litti over coal every afternoon and serve it with fresh chokha, so locals and visitors can taste it the traditional way.
